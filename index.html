Superpowers — keys 1 to 7
Every superpower is aimed. They land where your crosshair is, not on your own tank — put the cursor on a hostile, a group or a structure and fire. On touch and gamepad the turret direction is the aim line. A power aimed through a wall detonates against it.
1   BOOSTER BOMB
Cluster salvo lobbed onto your mark — a centre charge plus a ring that cascades outward.
2   FREEZE STRIKE
Cryo burst centred on your mark. Freezes tanks and seizes up emplacements caught in it.
3   EXPLOSIVE SHOTS
Instant three-round explosive burst at your mark, then your shells detonate on impact for 12s.
4   HOMING MISSILE
Locks whatever is nearest your mark and holds that lock. Flies to the mark if nothing is there.
5   TIME BOMB
Lobbed onto your mark. Long fuse, very large yield — a chokepoint and siege tool.
6   ATOMIC STRIKE
Calls a devastating strike down on your mark. Never lands on your own tile.
7   GUARDIAN DRONE
Deploys to your mark and holds that ground, then falls back to escorting you. 16s.
Charges are earned by fighting — kill streaks, cleared waves and cleared sectors all restock them. The rack in the top-right corner shows every power, its key, its remaining charges and its cooldown.

Team Operations
BASE ASSAULT
Two headquarters on one arena — yours in blue, theirs in green. Level the hostile HQ before they level yours. Both sides reinforce from their own HQ while it still stands, so the objective is the structure, not the body count. Explosives are the siege tool — bombs, the Time Bomb and the Atomic Strike do far more to a structure than cannon fire. A battered HQ also reinforces more slowly, so pressure compounds.
TEAM BATTLE
Squad against squad, no bases. First side wiped out loses. No reinforcements — every tank counts.
TANKS PER SIDE
2 to 5, set in the Operation tab. Both squads always field the same number and draw from the same roster.
ALLIED TANKS
Blue-steel livery with a chevron above the hull. They hunt, flank, take cover and call their own targets — your shells pass straight through them.
Arenas
Sectors rotate through twelve locations and eight arena shapes — grid, diamond, rotunda, wedge, pentagon, hexagon, octagon and crossroad. Alongside brick you will meet reinforced stone (tougher, needs a real blast) and watchtowers, which never fall and block line of sight.
Doctrine
Brick walls break — steel walls ricochet your shells (1 bounce). Use bounces around corners. Bombs excavate brick and chain-detonate barrels and mines. Kills within 4s build a combo multiplier up to ×6. Destroyed hostiles drop field supplies: repair, shield, rapid, tri-shot, overdrive, bombs, EMP. The AI Director watches you — play well and it sharpens the opposition.
